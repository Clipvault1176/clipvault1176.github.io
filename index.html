Privacy Policy

Effective 13 October 2026

This policy covers the ClipVault website and the ClipVault publishing software ("the App"), which connects only to ClipVault's own social media accounts.

Who uses the App

The App is used only by the ClipVault account owner to publish videos to accounts they own. It is not offered to the public and does not let other people sign in.

Information we access

  • When the account owner connects a platform (TikTok, Instagram or YouTube), the App receives an access token and basic profile details for that account, such as the account ID and username.
  • The App reads publishing options for the account (for example allowed privacy settings) and basic performance figures for our own videos, such as view counts.

How we use it

Only to upload ClipVault's videos and captions to ClipVault's own accounts and to see how those videos perform. We do not sell, rent or share this information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Storage and security

Access tokens are stored on the account owner's own computer and are never published. They can be revoked at any time from each platform's settings.

Viewers of our videos

We do not collect personal information from people who watch our videos or visit this website. Platforms such as TikTok, Instagram and YouTube handle viewer data under their own privacy policies.
